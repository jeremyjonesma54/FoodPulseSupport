What the app does

FoodPulse Insight is a food and wellbeing journal for iPhone and Apple Watch. It helps people review available heart-rate observations near meals and verified nutrition information. It does not measure blood glucose, diagnose conditions, or establish that a food caused a change in heart rate.

Information stored on your device

You may save meal names, ingredients, portions, photos, notes, timestamps, nutrition selections, meal history, experiments, personal preferences and related heart-rate summaries on your device. With your permission, FoodPulse reads available Apple Health measurements such as heart rate, steps, workouts and relevant weight or sleep information for personal displays. Apple Health permission may be denied or changed in iOS Settings.

Third-party services and optional data sharing

Core food journaling works without sending your complete meal history or HealthKit database to a FoodPulse-operated account service. When you explicitly choose optional services, a search phrase or barcode may be sent to USDA FoodData Central or a configured nutrition gateway for lookup. Optional cloud food recognition can send a resized selected photo to OpenAI. Optional cloud coaching can send the question you enter together with a bounded aggregate meal/nutrition summary to OpenAI. These third-party services may process submitted data and network metadata under their own privacy terms. Cloud AI is not required for core logging. Confirm exact services and data flows for the released version.

Backups, deletion and security

FoodPulse no longer creates automatic iCloud Documents backups of health records. The app offers a user-initiated, password-protected portable export of recorded meals and linked content using AES-GCM encryption. You choose where to store that file. Keep it somewhere private, outside iCloud Drive for health records, and protect its password; FoodPulse cannot recover the password. The app can import older, unencrypted format-12 backups on your explicit request. Older iCloud copies made by previous TestFlight releases are not automatically deleted or retrieved by the production app; you manage these historical copies separately.

You can edit or remove saved meal entries inside the app. Deleting an entry does not erase copies previously exported or backed up. Removing the app deletes local records according to iOS behavior, while copies held by the user or third-party services remain subject to their separate controls.

Advertising and analytics

Health information is not used for advertising or marketing. Verify the final App Store privacy questionnaire, network proxy behavior, and bundled libraries before claiming that no data is collected by third parties.

Children and health advice

FoodPulse is a general wellness journal and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Intended age range and age-rating answers are governed by the final App Store listing and relevant law.
