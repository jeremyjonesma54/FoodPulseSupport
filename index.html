FoodPulse Insight Support

Need help? Email . Include your iPhone and iOS versions, whether you use Apple Watch, the screen name, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not send unredacted medical/health data, meal photos, passwords, API keys or full backup files.

Getting started

  1. Open Today and select Log a meal.
  2. Confirm the actual ingredients and serving size.
  3. Optionally allow Apple Health reading permissions in Personal setup.
  4. Start and end a meal session on a paired Apple Watch if available.
  5. Review the meal after the two-hour observation window; some measurements may be unavailable.

Missing heart-rate data

Check iPhone Health permissions, Watch pairing and whether readings were recorded in the relevant period. Missing data cannot be inferred or backfilled by FoodPulse.

Backup and restore

Use Help & Privacy → Encrypted backup and restore. Choose a password of at least 12 characters; store your exported file securely outside iCloud Drive. Old format-12 JSON exports can still be imported. Never share your backup or password with support.

Privacy and limitations

FoodPulse is not a medical device. Heart-rate observations cannot diagnose conditions or blood glucose changes. Read the FoodPulse Privacy Policy.
